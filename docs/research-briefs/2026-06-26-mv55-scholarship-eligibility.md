## Ship-ready scholarship facts

### 1. Australia Awards Scholarship (Nepal) — fully funded, Master's
- **Nepal eligible: YES** — dedicated Nepal intake; must be a Nepali citizen applying from Nepal. (DFAT Nepal intake PDF)
- **Eligibility:** ≥18 yrs on 1 Feb of commencement year; not an Australian citizen/PR; 4-yr bachelor + ≥3 yrs relevant full-time work after the degree, OR 3-yr bachelor + ≥1-yr master's; English (valid 1 Jan of intake yr): IELTS Academic ≥6.5 (no band <6.0) / TOEFL iBT ≥84 / PTE Academic ≥58. (DFAT Nepal intake PDF + Policy Handbook)
- **Priority fields:** Climate Change, Disaster Risk Reduction, Economic Development, Education, Gender Equality/Disability/Social Inclusion.
- **Apply:** online via **OASIS**; shortlist → interview. (DFAT how-to-apply)
- **Dates (2027 intake):** opens 1 Feb 2026, closes 30 Apr 2026.
- **Covers:** full tuition, return airfare, establishment + living allowance, OSHC, academic support. **Condition:** must leave Australia ≥2 yrs after the award or incur a debt.

### 2. Destination Australia Program — regional, partial
- **Nepal eligible: YES** (international + domestic; nationality not a barrier).
- **Eligibility:** study at an approved **regional** campus; any level; prioritises skills-shortage fields. (education.gov.au/destination-australia)
- **Apply:** directly through an approved provider (the university/TAFE), not the government.
- **Dates:** provider-specific — no single national window (UNVERIFIED per-provider).
- **Covers:** up to **AUD 15,000/year**, renewable up to 4 yrs.

### 3. Research Training Program (RTP) — HDR (PhD / research master's)
- **Nepal eligible: YES** (funds domestic + overseas HDR; allocated by universities).
- **Eligibility:** enrolled/commencing research doctorate or research master's; strong academic + research record; merit-awarded by the university.
- **Apply:** via the host university's HDR admission + scholarship process (often automatic on admission).
- **Covers:** tuition-fee offset, living stipend, ancillary allowances. (e.g. Sydney RTP stipend AUD 42,754 p.a. 2026.)

### 4. University of Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarship (GRS)
- **Nepal eligible: YES** (domestic + international, merit; 300+/yr to international graduate researchers).
- **Eligibility:** meet graduate-research entry requirements; merit — **no separate application**, automatic on applying for the research course.
- **Apply:** apply directly to UniMelb for the PhD/research master's.
- **Covers:** living allowance **AUD 39,500/yr** (2026), full fee remission (up to 4 yrs PhD), OSHC (single), relocation grant AUD 3,000 from overseas.

### 5. University of Sydney — international research scholarships (RTP / USydIS)
- **Nepal eligible: YES** (international HDR).
- **Eligibility:** commencing/enrolled international HDR; outstanding academic record + prior research; automatic consideration on meeting HDR admission.
- **Apply:** apply for HDR admission via Sydney Courses + submit the scholarship form by the timeline.
- **Covers:** tuition offset + stipend (RTP Stipend AUD 42,754 p.a. 2026).
