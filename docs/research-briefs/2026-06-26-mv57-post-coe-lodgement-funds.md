## SHIP-READY (official sources)

### A. Lodge the subclass 500 Student visa
1. **Apply online via ImmiAccount** — all subclass 500 applications are lodged through ImmiAccount; documents (incl. GS responses) attach there. — DHA student-500 listing; studyaustralia.gov.au visa-application-process (accessed 2026-06-26)
2. **Attach core documents** — valid passport, CoE, OSHC policy, GS responses, English-test evidence, financial-capacity evidence. — same
3. **Answer the Genuine Student (GS) questions in the form** (applications on/after 23 Mar 2024): four prompts (ties/circumstances; why this course+provider; how it benefits you; other info), **150-word limit per response, in English**; assessed under **Ministerial Direction No. 106**. — immi.homeaffairs.gov.au …/student-500/genuine-student-requirement (fetched cleanly, accessed 2026-06-26)
4. **Financial capacity** — **AUD 29,710** for 12 months' living costs (from 10 May 2024), plus course fees + travel (partner +AUD 10,394, child +AUD 4,449). May be requested during processing even if not required at lodgement. — studyaustralia.gov.au financial-capacity news (accessed 2026-06-26) ⚠ re-verify amount near fiscal boundary
5. **OSHC compulsory** for the whole stay, starting before the course; no proof = refusal. — studyaustralia.gov.au OSHC (accessed 2026-06-26)
6. **Health examination + biometrics if requested** — outside Australia via approved panel physician (eMedical); biometrics collected in South Asia. — immi.homeaffairs.gov.au health + biometrics (accessed 2026-06-26)
7. **Visa Application Charge — AUD 2,000** primary applicant (since 1 Jul 2025; addl applicant 18+ AUD 1,225, under-18 AUD 400). — studyaustralia.gov.au VAC-increase news (accessed 2026-06-26) ⚠ re-verify against homeaffairs "current visa pricing"
8. **Processing time (Higher Education stream):** 50% within ~34 days, 90% within ~7 months. — immi.homeaffairs.gov.au global-visa-processing-times (accessed 2026-06-26) ⚠ live tool, changes often — re-verify

### B. After lodging
9. Wait; **don't travel until granted**; respond promptly to document/health/biometric requests; keep CoE active and OSHC valid; don't lodge a second visa to "speed it up." — DHA student-500 + processing-times pages (accessed 2026-06-26)

### C. Releasing funds — the one official anchor
10. **Get the NOC first** — No Objection Certificate from **MoEST** (noc.moest.gov.np); Nepali banks may not remit tuition/living costs without it. — noc.moest.gov.np (accessed 2026-06-26)

### D. Pre-departure
11. **Activate OSHC** so cover starts before course start; carry the policy card. — studyaustralia.gov.au OSHC (accessed 2026-06-26)
12. **Arrival:** carry CoE, eVisa grant (VEVO check), OSHC, funds evidence. — same
