MyVisa is a trust-first platform for international students to understand their real chances of studying abroad before engaging consultancies or spending money.

**Core problem:** Students face conflicting advice, outdated visa info, and overconfident promises from consultancies. They discover problems only after significant spend.

**What it does:** From a single comprehensive student profile, generate personalized assessments across 4 dimensions:
1. Academic eligibility — grades vs. admission requirements
2. Financial readiness — sponsorship, income, funds, loans, assets, evidence
3. Visa risk — study gaps, employment history, refusals, travel history, documentation strength, country-specific rules
4. Profile strength — achievements, work experience, leadership, research, volunteering

**Verdicts:** Strong Match / Possible / Reach — never misleading percentages.

**Key outputs:**
- University & program recommendations personalized to goals (PR pathway vs. rankings vs. research)
- Document checklist with progress tracking
- Personalized study-abroad roadmap with timeline and milestones
- Personalized feed (recommendations, scholarships, visa updates, deadlines)
- AI guide that explains reasoning, not decides — rule-based first, AI explains

**UX flow:** Lightweight wizard → immediate results (before registration) → dashboard as study-abroad command centre after sign-up.

**Why:** line: Students need honest answers to "Given my background, finances, goals, and circumstances, what is the smartest path to study abroad?" — without hidden incentives.

**How to apply:** Every feature decision should prioritize transparency (sourced rules, explained reasoning), trust (no overpromising), and progressive value (free value before signup). The long-term moat is anonymous outcome data turning rule-based scoring into data-driven predictions.
