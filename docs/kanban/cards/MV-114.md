## What this is

Founder-reported after a live look: on the **Your assessment** sample-profile
toggle (Aarav ⇄ Shruti), "the button to switch … aren't spaced properly and the
animation is laggy." Both were real; both were invisible to the jsdom suite (no
layout engine, no perceived-latency assertion). Presentational only.

## The two issues

### 1. Spacing — pills touched at 0px

`.mv-landing .toggle` (the segmented pill control) had `padding:3px` but **no
`gap`**. Its two `.toggle-opt` children are fully-rounded (`border-radius:999px`)
pills, so with zero gap they sat flush and the active plum pill was jammed against
its neighbour — reading as cramped / "not spaced properly."

**Fix:** `.mv-landing .toggle{gap:3px}` — the two pills now have even breathing
room (3px to the frame, 3px between).

### 2. Lag — the clicked pill waited 150ms

In `components/marketing/verdict-panel.tsx`, `select()` delayed `setActiveId(id)`
by 150ms (to crossfade the content), but `activeId` drove **both** the pill
highlight (`.on` + radio `checked`) **and** `getProfile(activeId)` content. So the
pill you clicked stayed unresponsive for the full 150ms before lighting up.

The v7 reference does not do this: it flips `aria-pressed` on the clicked button
**synchronously** and only delays the content `render()`.

**Fix:** split state.
- `selectedId` — set synchronously in `select()`; drives the pill `.on` class and
  the radio `checked`. Instant tactile feedback.
- `activeId` — still swapped after the 150ms crossfade; drives the verdict / dims /
  animated cost.
- The no-op guard became `if (id === selectedId) return`.
- A `swapRef` holds the swap `setTimeout` id, cleared on a rapid re-tap (so the
  now-instant pill can't flicker against a stale in-flight swap) and on unmount.

### Incidental

The copy-integrity guard (`tests/marketing/copy-integrity.test.ts`, which scans

new code comment → changed to en-dash per the design language (invariant: no
em-dashes).
