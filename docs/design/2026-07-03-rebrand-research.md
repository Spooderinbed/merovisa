# Mobbin rebrand research digest (MV-82 — Overhaul Phase 0a)

**Date:** 2026-07-03 · **Card:** `docs/kanban/cards/MV-82-mobbin-rebrand-research.md` · **Spec:** `docs/design/2026-07-03-elevated-calm-overhaul-spec.md`
**Feeds:** MV-83 (palette candidates), MV-84 (design-system v2), MV-85 (mascot brief).

## Method

Live Mobbin MCP sweep (fresh session, tools verified 2026-07-03). One research agent per app examined actual Mobbin screenshots — every observation cites the screen it came from (`mobbin.com/screens|flows/...`). Ten apps: Phantom (the pole), Linear, Headspace, Airbnb, Notion, Wise, Revolut, Cleo, Cash App, and Duolingo (**flow mechanics only** — its visual style is explicitly out of scope per the card). Surfaces swept per app: landing/marketing, onboarding quiz/wizard, sign-up/auth, results/score reveal, conversion prompts, empty states, error states, dark-mode handling.

**Platform: web is the primary evidence base.** The sweep initially ran against iOS captures; mid-run the founder directed that research target **websites, not mobile flows** (MyVisa currently ships only as a website — mobile research is parked for later). The body of this digest is therefore built from `platform: "web"` captures (plus Mobbin website sections for landing/marketing surfaces); the completed iOS pass is preserved in the appendix, clearly quarantined, and does not steer the synthesis or the MV-83/84/85 implications. Where an app's web presence on Mobbin is thin (some of these products are mobile-first), the gap is declared honestly rather than backfilled from iOS.

Honesty rules applied throughout: durations are only reported where evident (Mobbin returns stills — timing notes are choreography inferences, marked as such); coverage gaps are declared per app rather than guessed around.

- **Dark mode / B2B** — near-black hero "The wallet solution designed to connect at scale", the word "connect" in lavender, white + dark-gray pill pair, lavender floor glow ([screen](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0881026c-ca19-43b2-937a-39f37cbb10b9)).
- **Error** — light 404: plum "Page not found", gray line, soft-lavender pill, no mascot ([screen](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/85b90b34-bee4-45e1-8257-c5beb7e994b4)); dark 404 adds the copy-only ghost pun ([screen](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/71a72197-c5fa-4cf7-99ee-17282f2992a8)).
- **Footer** — lavender band with one small lone ghost, then a pale panel whose newsletter input is styled as a giant gray display-type placeholder "Enter your email" with a dark-plum Sign up pill; four quiet link columns ([screen](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1a5e60a8-b79e-4b2b-9aef-7a1d30867b51)).
- **Blog / content** — warm paper, "Phantom 👻 blog", chip-based filter system (Read/Watch, Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced with colored dots, topic chips), one big rounded hero card with 3D purple collage art ([screen](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/66845ca9-71a3-4268-b2d3-c2a670c029eb)).
- **Onboarding / Auth (wallet create-import) / Reveal / Empty states** — no Phantom web captures exist on Mobbin; see Coverage gaps. Not substituted from iOS per brief.

**Calm/premium mechanisms:** Each section is one idea — a single huge medium-weight headline, one gray sentence, one pill — with saturation strictly budgeted: the brand lavender only goes full-bleed at conversion moments, so the CTA band itself is the emphasis device and no urgency copy is needed. The mascot is disciplined to glyph scale inside sentences (and withheld entirely on error and B2B surfaces), which reads as wit under restraint rather than mascot theater. Geometry does the rest: everything actionable is a pill, everything else is a big-radius card on a barely-tinted ground, and dark surfaces are deployed editorially (stats, B2B) with the accent unchanged, giving gravitas without a second palette.

**Coverage gaps (web):**

- No Phantom product-UI screens on Mobbin web at all — extension/web-app onboarding, wallet create/import (auth), recovery-phrase reveal moment, empty states, and in-product conversion prompts are uncatalogued for web (3 screen queries + 2 flow queries all returned other apps: Family, Klarna, Cursor, Wise, Quicken, etc.).
- No Phantom web flows (multi-step) in Mobbin's flow library.
- No in-product dark-mode evidence for web; dark-mode observations are marketing-site sections only.
- The lavender-vs-yellow nav Download pill could be a hover/scroll-theme state — stills cannot distinguish; flagged as inference.
- Yellow-accent captures suggest a newer brand iteration mid-rollout; capture set mixes at least two site generations (4-item vs 6-item nav).

## Synthesis

### Five patterns for "calm but Gen-Z" wizards (web evidence)

**1. The bare stage.** The strongest wizards strip the chrome to almost nothing and put one question per screen in enormous, friendly type — youth register comes from scale and warmth, not decoration. Airbnb's host wizard reduces chrome to logo + "Questions?" + "Save & exit", centers a single question under a ~44–48px heading ([basics step](https://mobbin.com/screens/e786776b-aef6-40e2-ab8a-c33f4d82a3cd), [full flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/390e0140-7492-495a-8669-6cbdb8f73655)); Wise runs sign-up as one-question-per-screen under a thin stepper ([setting up an account](https://mobbin.com/flows/db72a145-5398-4854-ad12-657e6eb2e505)); Notion's onboarding is a blank white field with one centered card and no step counter at all ([flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/7a12675f-80fc-4608-9feb-7ec412cf55c5)); Linear pages centered single questions with dot progress ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/cbaf58a9-fd84-4afe-b214-e65b24484013)). Duolingo confirms the mechanic: strictly one question, Continue disabled until an answer exists ([13-screen onboarding](https://mobbin.com/flows/849942d8-918e-4bb8-8a73-591eb2859d5f)).

**2. Progress lives at the edge.** Progress is a thin, persistent bar pinned to a screen edge that fills continuously within the active segment — never a modal, never a shouting counter. Airbnb pins a full-width segmented track to the bottom edge and fills it left→right inside the current macro-step ([basics step](https://mobbin.com/screens/e786776b-aef6-40e2-ab8a-c33f4d82a3cd) vs [step-2 interstitial](https://mobbin.com/screens/e8e9b940-5b4d-45db-a5dd-8260a2ceb3e9)); Wise fills thin dark-green top segments across both sign-up and the 4-step send tracker ([sign-up flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/db72a145-5398-4854-ad12-657e6eb2e505), [send flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/77c0a951-fd82-4d40-8146-e54549e9319b)); Notion's only progress affordance is one thin dark bar ([Choose your Plan flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/6a0c0bb0-ec58-437a-be74-66a9f683b860)); Duolingo's persistent top bar spans quiz and lesson alike ([quiz step](https://mobbin.com/screens/fe47e553-ea8d-40b2-8176-91063c68439b)) — mechanics only.

**3. Answer in, acknowledgment out.** Every input gets an immediate, legible response: the primary button's state change is the micro-reward, and section boundaries get a dedicated acknowledgment beat. Headspace's Continue pill goes translucent→solid black the moment any option is picked ([unselected](https://mobbin.com/screens/c8499f67-c5a8-4863-a2dd-c55e89c114fc) vs [selected](https://mobbin.com/screens/287e362d-6359-4953-882a-0ca03e0c23ff)), then paces quiz sections with full-screen "Thanks for sharing how you're doing" interstitials ([interstitial](https://mobbin.com/screens/87a1d76d-7533-4f3e-83a2-7e6ca33fe681)); Airbnb answers a completed macro-step with a narrative interstitial — the isometric room literally furnishes itself as you progress ([listing flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/390e0140-7492-495a-8669-6cbdb8f73655)); Duolingo (mechanics) reflects each answer back as a personalized value line ("That's 25 words in your first week!") before asking the next question ([18-screen onboarding](https://mobbin.com/flows/2c317632-13c2-4313-9977-98ac58c0d9d4)). This is the calm substitute for gamified reward: acknowledgment, not points.

**4. Disarm at the anxiety point.** Trust microcopy is placed exactly where fear spikes, in quiet gray type, not in a badge wall. Airbnb puts "You won't be charged yet" directly under Reserve and "Your address is only shared after a reservation" under the location question ([booking flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/be988574-2b30-4b82-a5a3-30352abbf1a2), [wizard flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/390e0140-7492-495a-8669-6cbdb8f73655)); Wise pre-explains intrusiveness ("By law, we may need to ask for proof of your address" at the country step) and itemises "Total fees: Free — no fees to pay" receipt-style ([sign-up flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/db72a145-5398-4854-ad12-657e6eb2e505), [send flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/77c0a951-fd82-4d40-8146-e54549e9319b)); Headspace de-fangs its trial with a vertical Today / In 5 days / In 7 days timeline ([subscribe flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/8f019fc7-7742-42f9-b0bb-c3046ccd40c4)); Cash App styles blunt fee disclosure as plain gray body text in a hairline FAQ ([common questions](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/13a1b5d5-0446-4220-b05d-d7a94c61ad8f)). Candor set quietly reads as confidence.

**5. The reveal is the receipt.** No app in the calm set celebrates with confetti; the payoff artifact itself is the celebration. Wise ends a transfer on a quiet vertical timeline with check ticks — the completed receipt is the reveal ([transaction details](https://mobbin.com/screens/ea2fde3e-8b3e-4ef8-92d9-ea5dc9e51fda)); Linear's only ceremony is the typographic "That was easy!" before dropping you into a pre-seeded workspace ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/cbaf58a9-fd84-4afe-b214-e65b24484013)); Notion skips celebration entirely — the reveal is a pre-filled "Welcome to Notion" doc with a populated sidebar ([flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/6a0c0bb0-ec58-437a-be74-66a9f683b860)). Duolingo (mechanics) shows how to pace it: drip-feed one conclusion per screen with any conversion ask adjacent to — never blocking — the result ([lesson complete with rail](https://mobbin.com/screens/d9f66e6b-8e2a-488a-be32-e85078ae2666)). For a verdict wizard: stage the result as a sequence of single-purpose screens ending in a substantive artifact, with the account ask beside it.

### Three tells of premium fintech-calm (web evidence)

**1. One saturated element per screen.** Every premium-calm app spends its brand hue like money: exactly one saturated element marks "the thing you do next" and everything else is neutral. Linear's violet appears only on the single primary action, with status colour quarantined to 12px donuts and chips ([Plans](https://mobbin.com/screens/3b233553-84e9-4c59-adf7-e8ec5991b245), [issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4e0a8da-a387-49c7-8940-b64521bd3809)); Notion permits one blue element per screen — even its pricing page's sole emphasis is an inverted black button ([login](https://mobbin.com/screens/c10168a0-730b-4a72-933c-22614ff4c648), [pricing cards](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b2f27058-0178-424f-aef6-d32ba5ab490e)); Wise budgets its loud lime to one pill per view while forest green does the structural work ([home](https://mobbin.com/flows/5ee4db65-7476-48c2-903a-e8f28b0d4c00)); Airbnb reserves its red-pink gradient for commitment CTAs and lets near-black do all routine progression ([auth Continue](https://mobbin.com/screens/e0a010cf-5be7-4690-a5bd-0ac5dab3d658), [booking flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/be988574-2b30-4b82-a5a3-30352abbf1a2)). Two saturated elements competing on one screen is the tell of non-premium.

**2. Numbers are the typography.** In money-grade UI the figures themselves carry hierarchy: giant, near-black numerals over receipt-style hairline rows whose small-print amounts are bolded. Wise sets "recipient gets" at ~40–56px bold over an itemised fee list, and keeps the zero honest ("0.00 SGD") even on empty states ([send flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/77c0a951-fd82-4d40-8146-e54549e9319b), [empty balance](https://mobbin.com/screens/ef617d17-16af-473d-847f-cef12f0c81fa)); Revolut runs bold ~28px balances against 11–12px gray captions in a compact 4-step scale ([home](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd272e80-ed27-4fcd-b643-5a1f66dee8ba)); Cash App gives FAQ questions display size with hairline dividers and leaves the 3% / 0.5–1.75% fee facts in quiet gray body text ([FAQ](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/13a1b5d5-0446-4220-b05d-d7a94c61ad8f)). Disclosure typeset like a bank statement — not persuasion — is what makes high-stakes screens feel audited. Directly transferable to verdict bands and cost figures.

**3. Texture is quarantined to artwork; chrome stays dead-flat.** Premium fintechs permit gradients, metallics, 3D and multicolor only inside contained art, never on UI surfaces. Revolut's metallic card renders and glowing orbs read as jewelry precisely because buttons, cards and inputs stay flat white/gray ([plans tiles](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3e5806d2-ff17-40b6-9743-5f611167786a), [confirm modal](https://mobbin.com/screens/9e32f8ff-dde3-454c-a428-3d301ee23f18)); Linear separates everything with hairline borders and 2–3% tint shifts instead of shadows ([issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4e0a8da-a387-49c7-8940-b64521bd3809)); Phantom confines multicolor to contained sticker-collage artwork while UI chrome stays lavender + plum ([trading carousel](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/10765f4c-84d9-45f8-9fcd-8ba8d6a6ddf8)); Cash App's iridescent chrome flowers live only on the footer band ([green footer](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6a949fdc-0731-45dd-b8ae-ecd5c4a4386d)); Wise's watercolour objects mark waiting moments and never touch the fee math ([send flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/77c0a951-fd82-4d40-8146-e54549e9319b)). Flat surfaces + hairlines + one contained art moment = the material recipe.

## Implications for MV-83 / MV-84 / MV-85

### For MV-83 (palette candidates)

- **① Night indigo — proven-calm but the most crowded lane in the studied set.** Three of the ten apps already occupy indigo-violet on web: Linear's muted ~#5E6AD2 ([Plans](https://mobbin.com/screens/3b233553-84e9-4c59-adf7-e8ec5991b245)), Revolut's ~#5C5CE0 periwinkle ([add money](https://mobbin.com/screens/ff0f3124-3117-4b9a-9154-e0b3bebcc887)), Phantom's ~#ab9ff2 lavender ([hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/71259123-3051-49c8-afc1-6a3afaa222f9)). Evidence supports the *mechanics* — dampened saturation reads calm (Linear deliberately periwinkle-not-electric, disabled states desaturate further to pale lavender, [disabled Continue](https://mobbin.com/screens/fa5eecc4-532f-4cc2-a6e8-56814eefbf89)) — but ownership is weak: within this set indigo-violet is the default "premium tool/fintech" hue. Dark-mode derivation is its strongest card: Phantom holds the identical lavender accent on both lilac-white and near-black grounds ([light 404](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/85b90b34-bee4-45e1-8257-c5beb7e994b4) vs [dark 404](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/71a72197-c5fa-4cf7-99ee-17282f2992a8)) — a mid-saturation indigo is the cheapest hue to carry across modes unchanged.
- **② Deep blue — validated as an action colour, which is exactly the warning.** Headspace routes every action through saturated royal blue (~#0061ef) while brand warmth lives in orange ([hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6f9a7150-b5d0-4426-af42-a2662ffbb003)); Notion's one functional blue (~#2383E2) is its only interactive hue ([login](https://mobbin.com/screens/c10168a0-730b-4a72-933c-22614ff4c648)). Both prove blue reads sober and trustworthy — but both use it precisely because it is *anonymous*: it's the hue apps choose when they want the interaction layer to disappear behind the brand. As a brand primary, the corporate-generic risk is confirmed, not just suspected. Practical warns: no studied app runs full-sat royal blue on dark grounds, so dark mode needs a lightened variant; and glacier-cool paper has thinner precedent than warm paper in this set (only Airbnb and Linear run cool/dead-neutral grounds; Wise, Headspace, Cash App and Revolut's marketing all sit on warm paper).
- **③ Dusk plum — the open lane, with one adjacency to test.** No studied app owns plum/berry as a primary; the nearest neighbours are Phantom's dark desaturated plum used only as *headline ink* ([hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/71259123-3051-49c8-afc1-6a3afaa222f9)) and Airbnb's hotter red-pink rationed to commitment CTAs ([auth](https://mobbin.com/screens/e0a010cf-5be7-4690-a5bd-0ac5dab3d658)) — ownership confirmed. Warm sand pairing has the strongest neutral precedent of the three (Wise greige ~#F2F1EC [dashboard](https://mobbin.com/flows/77c0a951-fd82-4d40-8146-e54549e9319b), Headspace ~#f7f2ea [hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6f9a7150-b5d0-4426-af42-a2662ffbb003), Cash App warm greige [hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f148f488-39dc-4369-9385-912e6e40d157), Revolut marketing ~#f4f2ed [plans](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3e5806d2-ff17-40b6-9743-5f611167786a)). Warning: plum sits closest to the Reach red. The whole set reserves red strictly for genuine failure (Revolut [failed deposit](https://mobbin.com/screens/082268c6-c6b1-42c6-8997-84561d5b9488), Notion inline errors, Headspace payment errors) — the primary must keep enough blue/violet distance from the #b1503a-class Reach hue that a primary button never whispers "error"; run a primary-vs-Reach adjacency test as a gate. Dark mode is the costliest of the three: expect a lifted/brightened dark variant (the same move as the current teal→#4eb39f), since dark plum on near-black has no precedent on file.
- **Recommended saturation strategy: two-pole rationing.** Mid-saturation primary spent on exactly one action per screen (Linear, Notion, Wise, Revolut — see synthesis tell 1), plus a near-white tint of the same hue for selected/secondary/disabled surfaces (Revolut's ~#ECECFB lavender tint system [disabled CTA](https://mobbin.com/screens/e1754447-3b91-494b-ad7e-5904d09b9893); Phantom's lilac near-white reading surfaces [hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/71259123-3051-49c8-afc1-6a3afaa222f9)). Full-saturation floods only as ceremony surfaces — conversion/ritual moments, never ambient (Phantom [download band](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/eb72a719-fa30-4311-8c31-22b11266a89b), Headspace [quiz sky](https://mobbin.com/screens/287e362d-6359-4953-882a-0ca03e0c23ff), Cash App all-or-nothing green [Newsroom hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/158546fe-dd48-403a-99a5-2219a8ed7f2b)).
- **Neutral ramp: spec 5 steps per mode + a hairline value.** Observed web range is 4–6 distinguishable steps everywhere: Linear 4–5, Notion 5–6, Wise 4, Headspace 4, Airbnb 5, Revolut 5, Phantom 5, Cash App 4. Separation by hairline border and 2–3% tint shift, never shadow (Linear [issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4e0a8da-a387-49c7-8940-b64521bd3809), Airbnb hairline system [booking flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/be988574-2b30-4b82-a5a3-30352abbf1a2)).
- **Accent-count discipline: the verdict trio consumes the entire accent budget.** Fintech norm is 2–3 functional accents beyond primary (Notion 2–3, Revolut ~2, Wise ~2, Airbnb ~3); Linear's 5–6 survive only because they're confined to 12px micro scale ([dark issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/e142df2a-3527-499c-8f81-1b715947ac0c)). Strong green / Possible amber / Reach red map 1:1 to the semantic accents the set already uses (Linear amber in-progress / green done / red urgent; Headspace green success / red error) — so: zero decorative accents on top, and the primary must be chromatically distant from all three verdict hues. This rules out any green-family primary (Wise's forest green would collide with Strong) and favors indigo/blue/plum.
- **Verdict-trio coexistence rules from evidence:** verdict hues live at band/chip scale on neutral ground, never as large fills (Linear's quarantine — no accent ever appears as a large fill); the primary never appears inside the semantic system (Headspace's blue-actions vs green/yellow/red-semantics split is the cleanest model on file, [payment](https://mobbin.com/screens/fe82e6db-108b-451f-9dc3-9da8e71ca0c5)).
- **Dark-mode derivation: re-picked, not inverted — unanimous.** Phantom re-inks the same layout ([404 pair](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/71a72197-c5fa-4cf7-99ee-17282f2992a8)); Linear re-establishes light's sidebar/canvas relationships on a re-picked near-black rather than flipping ([dark issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/e142df2a-3527-499c-8f81-1b715947ac0c)); Revolut and Cash App dark sections place re-picked components, not darkened ones ([dark plan cards](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/cac16ee8-d037-4b22-8de1-5eedc7457b1a), [dark footer](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/155762f6-11fc-4665-800e-0da331b8304e)). Only Linear proves a full product dark theme on web — offered as a first-class choice at onboarding step 2 ([theme picker](https://mobbin.com/screens/efc1c7a1-c215-4de7-be32-4f68a1bb3e20)). Whichever candidate wins, define dark as its own 5-step neutral ramp + accent re-check, not a token inversion; ① is cheapest here, ③ costliest.

### For MV-84 (design-system v2)

- **Radius scale: a 3-tier ladder + pill, split into two registers.** Observed convention: inputs ~6–10px, cards ~12–16px, modals/panels ~16–24px, and 999px pills for interactive elements (Wise "every button is a pill" [send flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/77c0a951-fd82-4d40-8146-e54549e9319b); Headspace pill=interactive rule; Revolut pills in both product and marketing [home](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd272e80-ed27-4fcd-b643-5a1f66dee8ba); Cash App no rectangular buttons at all [hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f148f488-39dc-4369-9385-912e6e40d157)). Linear proves the register split: ceremonial pills outside the app shell (onboarding/auth [theme step](https://mobbin.com/screens/efc1c7a1-c215-4de7-be32-4f68a1bb3e20)), tight ~6px rects inside the dense workspace ([issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4e0a8da-a387-49c7-8940-b64521bd3809)). MyVisa's current 8/12/16/999 ladder sits inside the observed envelope — keep it, but codify the register rule: pills for wizard/auth/marketing ceremony, tighter radii on dense data surfaces.
- **Type: 2 working weights carry hierarchy on most webs.** Headspace runs a strict bold+regular pair ([hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6f9a7150-b5d0-4426-af42-a2662ffbb003)); Cash App ~2 weights with size doing the work at 64–90px display ([hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f148f488-39dc-4369-9385-912e6e40d157)); Airbnb stays in the regular–semibold band with a two-tone ink/#717171 split ([wizard](https://mobbin.com/flows/390e0140-7492-495a-8669-6cbdb8f73655)). The dense-product exception: weight + gray value at near-identical sizes (Linear [issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4e0a8da-a387-49c7-8940-b64521bd3809); Notion bold-title + same-size gray subtitle [login](https://mobbin.com/screens/c10168a0-730b-4a72-933c-22614ff4c648)). Spec: max 2–3 weights; display hierarchy via size jumps, row hierarchy via weight + gray; mono reserved for machine strings only (Linear login code [flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/2b9e0315-3654-482e-b877-f8da3736939f)) — matches IBM Plex Mono's current role. For verdicts and money: Wise's two-register model — giant bold figure over small receipt rows with bolded amounts ([send flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/77c0a951-fd82-4d40-8146-e54549e9319b)) — is the template for verdict words and cost figures.
- **Motion honesty note:** durations and easing are never observable from Mobbin stills — the evidence constrains *choreography*, not timing. The 0.4s / --ease-out-emphatic numbers are our choice; what follows validates the choreography vocabulary.
- **Step-change (slide-fwd/slide-back):** the ground never animates, only the center column swaps — Headspace's sky gradient persists across quiz steps while the question column changes ([quiz](https://mobbin.com/screens/287e362d-6359-4953-882a-0ca03e0c23ff)); Notion swaps discrete centered cards on an unchanging white field ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/7a12675f-80fc-4608-9feb-7ec412cf55c5)); Linear pages a dot-paged carousel ([flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/cbaf58a9-fd84-4afe-b214-e65b24484013)). Maps directly to slide-fwd/slide-back with a static page ground.
- **Progress + loading:** continuous fill within the active segment (Airbnb's bottom bar [basics](https://mobbin.com/screens/e786776b-aef6-40e2-ab8a-c33f4d82a3cd) vs [interstitial](https://mobbin.com/screens/e8e9b940-5b4d-45db-a5dd-8260a2ceb3e9)) = CSS width transition, no spinner; skeletons stage content arrival, not spinners (Airbnb [skeleton homepage](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/450775de-d3c1-43cd-8c7f-9c0a171ec00e), Revolut skeleton page mid-[login flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/266aca43-94df-4114-95e3-0b802d0bb28b)).
- **State-change as the highest-frequency micro-motion:** disabled→solid primary on validity is the one animation every app repeats — Headspace translucent→black Continue ([unselected](https://mobbin.com/screens/c8499f67-c5a8-4863-a2dd-c55e89c114fc)/[selected](https://mobbin.com/screens/287e362d-6359-4953-882a-0ca03e0c23ff)), Revolut pale-lavender→violet ([disabled](https://mobbin.com/screens/e1754447-3b91-494b-ad7e-5904d09b9893)), Airbnb gray→black Next ([OTP](https://mobbin.com/screens/e2875147-414a-4fd0-b1e1-cc4f22427b07)). rise-sm (6px/0.4s) belongs here and on card entrances — small, frequent, quiet.
- **"One reveal moment per page" is proven by:** Phantom — a single scramble count-up on one stats band, everything else static ([stats](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3f841a54-d4dd-414c-863a-97417d079161)); Headspace — one full-screen interstitial per quiz section ([interstitial](https://mobbin.com/screens/87a1d76d-7533-4f3e-83a2-7e6ca33fe681)); Airbnb — one narrative illustration beat per macro-step ([wizard flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/390e0140-7492-495a-8669-6cbdb8f73655)); Wise — the completion timeline as the sole reveal ([transaction details](https://mobbin.com/screens/ea2fde3e-8b3e-4ef8-92d9-ea5dc9e51fda)); Linear — one typographic "That was easy!" beat ([flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/cbaf58a9-fd84-4afe-b214-e65b24484013)). Direct evidence for one --ease-out-emphatic reveal per page.
- **Modal vs inline:** centered dialog + dim scrim for blocking decisions (Linear [delete confirm](https://mobbin.com/screens/c4e6a99e-5c6f-4d93-894a-f566b758608b), Notion [cancel modal](https://mobbin.com/screens/ba93ce22-6dd3-4eda-ace5-0b78d593f8e0), Revolut [confirm](https://mobbin.com/screens/9e32f8ff-dde3-454c-a428-3d301ee23f18)); non-blocking detail slides in as a right drawer instead of navigating away (Revolut [transaction drawer](https://mobbin.com/screens/cc6213e1-c3bd-4197-b8ab-b6686ff9c553)); success is a small corner toast (Linear [toast](https://mobbin.com/screens/fea37c46-ab06-4d46-86c7-333ffb0db455)). Prefer drawer/toast over modal wherever the user isn't deciding.
- **Staggers:** not directly observable in stills — no app shows evidence of long cascading entrances; grids arrive whole or via skeleton (Airbnb, Revolut above). The ≤5-sibling / 40–60ms cap is therefore a restraint rule consistent with the one-flourish evidence, not something the web set demonstrates — keep it, but treat it as our discipline, not a borrowed pattern.

### For MV-85 (mascot brief)

- **Zoning: doorways, not desks — three independent systems agree.** Phantom's ghost lives at glyph scale inside marketing headlines and one lone footer corner, and is withheld from the light 404 and the B2B hero entirely ([hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/71259123-3051-49c8-afc1-6a3afaa222f9), [footer](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1a5e60a8-b79e-4b2b-9aef-7a1d30867b51), [light 404](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/85b90b34-bee4-45e1-8257-c5beb7e994b4)). Headspace anchors characters small in the corners of emotional edges — login sun, 404 tangle, cancellation cat — and pointedly keeps them off pricing, payment, quiz answer pills and library chrome ([login](https://mobbin.com/screens/e2022433-e7a6-4a89-98c5-652ed9f6f9bc), [404](https://mobbin.com/screens/f8e16f35-df5d-4f58-bdd2-cb93ce432ada), [goodbye](https://mobbin.com/screens/e3cfbd87-54bb-4e62-b254-82bc358fbe2d)). Notion's ink characters appear only at thresholds — marketing hero, onboarding choice cards, 404, cancel modal — never on pricing tables, auth forms, settings or in-product empty states ([interest step](https://mobbin.com/flows/bf180963-e053-4aab-bab3-8e4ce633b888), [404](https://mobbin.com/screens/21fade9f-d41a-4ee5-8052-4d2803765a21), [empty library](https://mobbin.com/screens/efba4a5c-d108-4388-9210-12a819599453)). The founder-locked shell-only list matches these zones exactly.
- **The zero-mascot majority sets the default to "no bird".** Five apps in the set — Linear, Wise, Revolut, Airbnb, Cash App — run no character anywhere and still read premium; among the money apps specifically, mascot presence on product surfaces is zero (Wise's watercolour objects mark waiting/verification and never touch the fee math, [send flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/77c0a951-fd82-4d40-8146-e54549e9319b); Revolut product UI is pointedly illustration-free, [empty home](https://mobbin.com/screens/2a30f7f3-8edb-4f79-9faf-e6f7777f39f7)). Every bird appearance must earn its slot; when in doubt, omit.
- **Abstraction: legible at glyph scale, monochrome-first.** The working sizes are tiny — Phantom's ghost replaces a single word-beat inside a headline ([download CTA](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/eb72a719-fa30-4311-8c31-22b11266a89b)); Notion's 404 character is just a pair of ink eyes ([404](https://mobbin.com/screens/21fade9f-d41a-4ee5-8052-4d2803765a21)); Headspace's account avatar is a sleepy cloud blob ([settings](https://mobbin.com/screens/eb779774-44d9-4e03-9abe-dde5ad2ac9c8)). The bird should be a geometric silhouette that survives at 16–24px, mono or duotone, with at most one controlled colour moment (Notion's ink portrait with colored glyphs is the single colour explosion in its whole onboarding, [flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/bf180963-e053-4aab-bab3-8e4ce633b888)).
- **Mood-match, never cheerlead — and copy can substitute for the mascot.** Headspace's cancellation cat is wistful and its 404 figure is tangled — the character mirrors the user's state rather than selling ([goodbye](https://mobbin.com/screens/e3cfbd87-54bb-4e62-b254-82bc358fbe2d), [404](https://mobbin.com/screens/f8e16f35-df5d-4f58-bdd2-cb93ce432ada)); Phantom's dark 404 makes the ghost joke in copy alone ("We haven't ghosted you!") with the mascot withheld ([dark 404](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/71a72197-c5fa-4cf7-99ee-17282f2992a8)). On error pages the bird is optional — voice can carry the wit.
- **Empty states: smallest register only.** The calm set's product empties are text-only (Wise [empty balance](https://mobbin.com/screens/ef617d17-16af-473d-847f-cef12f0c81fa), Revolut [empty home](https://mobbin.com/screens/2a30f7f3-8edb-4f79-9faf-e6f7777f39f7)) or a tiny gray glyph (Notion [empty library](https://mobbin.com/screens/efba4a5c-d108-4388-9210-12a819599453)); Linear allows a small monochrome sketch only on feature-intro empties, while filtered/no-results empties stay pure text + recovery action ([Customers](https://mobbin.com/screens/f2e7538e-b21c-4933-a5d2-4e4c31853e88) vs [filtered](https://mobbin.com/screens/d3ab0127-662f-4764-9c84-511cd7345e27)). So: bird permitted on first-run/feature-intro empties only, small and monochrome; "no results" and zero-data document/checklist surfaces stay text-only.
- **Never list (grounded):** never on data/verdict/money surfaces (Wise keeps illustration off calculator, fees, review and KYC; Headspace off payment and plan chooser; Notion off all working surfaces); never at hero scale (Phantom never inflates the ghost into a hero illustration — its biggest render is a silhouette on card art); never as a progress or reward device (no calm-set app uses its character for progress; that's the banned gamification lane); never guilt-tripping at exits — Notion's cancel-trial dog beside red strikethrough feature names is the set's one loss-aversion character moment ([cancel modal](https://mobbin.com/screens/ba93ce22-6dd3-4eda-ace5-0b78d593f8e0)) and is off-thesis under no-shame; Headspace's melancholy-appropriate cat is the model instead.
- **Guide-avatar precedent is thin but real:** Headspace fronts its AI ("Ebb") as an abstract blob character on the 2025 landing proposition card ([landing flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/ff115024-a032-4315-87df-f2f25d550528)) — precedent for an abstract character representing the guide on marketing surfaces, but no web capture shows the character inside the chat product itself.
- **Honesty flag — web evidence on product-surface zoning is thin at both poles.** Phantom has zero product-UI web coverage (marketing sections only), Cleo has nil web coverage of any kind (a coverage fact only — no visual claims possible), and Headspace's signed-in surfaces appear mainly through content-thumbnail art. Treat the zoning rules above as "provisional (web)"; the iOS appendix carries the richer product-surface zoning signal — verify against mobile evidence when mobile ships, do not import it now.

**Calm/premium mechanisms:** Cash App earns confidence through subtraction with one loud constant: the keypad strips away every affordance (no key chrome, no labels, no card containers) so the giant numeral is the only thing that can matter, and the surrounding neon green reads as owned identity rather than decoration precisely because nothing else on the screen competes. Actions are disciplined into a two-tone system — one black pill per screen is "the" action, gray/green-on-green pills are everything else — so users never parse color semantics under money stress; red is spent only on genuinely destructive or failed states, keeping it credible. The typewriter-mono legal fine print and plain-sentence confirmations ("Sent! $1 will be deposited once…") borrow bank-document seriousness, so the emptiness reads as certainty, not absence.

| Mechanic | Observation |

| Onboarding quiz pacing | Strictly one question per screen with a persistent back arrow; the sampled onboarding flow runs ~20 screens total including signup. Question screens observed: language pick → proficiency self-report ("How much French do you know?", 5 self-assessment options) → daily-goal pick → "Where would you like to start?" Skip affordances exist on optional asks ("NOT NOW" on the widget ask, "LATER" on profile creation). Value before effort: the placement result ("Since you know a few words, let's start at Score 10!") and the quiz itself all happen **before** account creation — signup is asked only at the end, framed as "Create a profile to save your progress!" (protect what you already earned), then name/email/password come one field per screen. |
| Progress affordance | A thin top progress bar spans the whole onboarding including the signup fields, filling per answered step — signup reads as "finishing," not a new gate. In lessons, the same top-bar pattern fills per exercise; a combo counter escalates across consecutive correct answers ("17 IN A ROW") with milestone feedback flashes, and the bar's state visibly changes for the end-of-session mistake-review stretch, signalling the final lap. The home path shows one unlocked "next" node with everything downstream greyed — a single obvious next action. |
| Commitment mechanics | Commitment is elicited as a choice, not a toggle: the daily-goal screen offers 4 sized tiers (5/10/15/20 min/day, each with an identity label — Casual/Regular/Serious/Intense) and the CTA is the pledge itself: "I'M COMMITTED". | Error/retry mechanics | A wrong answer slides an inline result sheet over the exercise showing the correct answer immediately ("Incorrect — Correct Answer: ant") with a single low-blame "GOT IT" button — no instant forced retry and no restart; the session continues. The retry loop is deferred: missed items are re-queued later in the same session, explicitly labelled "PREVIOUS MISTAKE", so the learner re-attempts after a gap. Correct answers get an equivalent inline confirmation sheet ("Good job!" / "Awesome!") with CONTINUE, keeping right/wrong pacing symmetric. A hearts/energy counter sits in the lesson header as the stakes mechanic. |
| Results/reveal mechanics | Lesson-complete is a **sequence of single-purpose screens**, each with exactly one CTA. **Transferable mechanics:** The one-question-per-screen quiz with a single persistent progress bar that keeps filling *through* account creation transfers directly to an assessment wizard — signup reads as completing the assessment rather than a wall, and the "save your progress" framing (ask for the account only after the verdict-relevant answers exist) maps cleanly to the anonymous-results→account seam. The reveal choreography also transfers: lead with one qualitative verdict sentence, cap stats at ~3 on the verdict screen, and give every subsequent disclosure its own screen with exactly one next action. Deferred, labelled re-asks ("previous mistake" re-queued later, corrections shown immediately without blame or forced retry) are a calm error pattern for re-checking weak profile answers — all of this is pacing/sequencing, none of it requires points, streaks, or celebration aesthetics.

- The "Find my level" placement-test exercises themselves were not sampled — only the entry choice and the resulting "start at Score 10" outcome screen.
- Mobbin flow previews are evenly-spaced samples of longer flows (20+ screens), so intermediate onboarding questions between the sampled ones may exist unseen.

### iOS-evidence synthesis (superseded by the web synthesis above)

### Five patterns for "calm but Gen-Z" wizards

**1. Fixed frame, one decision.** The wizard chrome never moves; only the question swaps. Airbnb's 29–47-screen host wizard keeps a persistent "Save & exit" escape pill, a "Questions?" help pill, and a bottom segmented progress bar on every single step, with exactly one large conversational heading and one decision per screen ([29-screen flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/ac0a721e-274d-4b18-97eb-403b4c59b394), [property-type grid](https://mobbin.com/screens/fcf88e90-fe40-4e5e-bf0d-93a668aa291f)). Headspace holds a domed white sheet as a stable scaffold across quiz steps with only content swapping inside it ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/31b21791-dec6-448a-8253-648f5ebbba3e)); Linear runs a dot-paged carousel of centered single-question pages ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/cbaf58a9-fd84-4afe-b214-e65b24484013)); Duolingo (mechanics) is strictly one question per screen under one persistent top progress bar ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/b0b4f93f-5637-46ec-9d77-49ecda6b991d)). The stability is what makes 20+ steps feel low-stakes: each screen is a small turn inside an unchanging room.

**2. Progress swallows the ask.** Value and sunk effort come before every gate, and the gates are drawn as part of the same journey. Duolingo (mechanics) runs the quiz and placement result before signup, then keeps the same top progress bar filling *through* the account-creation fields so signup reads as finishing the assessment, not a wall — and parks the paywall at the very end with a visible "NO THANKS" ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/b0b4f93f-5637-46ec-9d77-49ecda6b991d), [profile-creation flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/9f5126ab-a403-4ac6-bc9f-1f44b1d373ec), [paywall](https://mobbin.com/screens/d383e614-83f8-430e-907d-5e91461aa7c9)). Headspace sequences identically — quiz, then named reveal, and only then the trial paywall with explicit charge date and "cancel anytime" ([paywall](https://mobbin.com/screens/9b458f77-dbe4-4b69-b6d8-e8f300e2d93e)); Linear lands new users in a pre-seeded workspace so the first minute after onboarding is never empty ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/cbaf58a9-fd84-4afe-b214-e65b24484013)); Phantom's fresh $0.00 wallet keeps full home structure with "Let's get started!" cards instead of a hollow state ([adding-wallet flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/22a88db4-5730-47b4-b51d-dd26028d28ec)).

**3. Personality stands down at stakes.** Every app with a voice or character mutes it exactly where anxiety peaks, and that withdrawal is what lets the personality exist at all. Phantom's ghost lives on splash, welcome, and username surfaces and is pointedly absent from every money and security screen ([splash](https://mobbin.com/screens/59958f74-06d2-4804-b81d-6d88c2cbf057), [send keypad](https://mobbin.com/screens/47237839-b82f-430d-add2-a42c46c80b3f)). Headspace's blobs withdraw from the sign-up form, the entire 10-question assessment, and the results score disc ([check-in flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/ce032b9a-cb9f-42f9-b407-19a2796c6961)). Cleo's swearing, meme-flinging chat voice goes fully silent on Plaid credentials, KYC, and the payment sheet — plain white, gray legal type, zero jokes ([Plaid credentials](https://mobbin.com/screens/ff64faaa-447a-4279-8d63-049b01c20a19), [set up wallet flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/c5e03c75-82c4-4053-8d88-7d0e64fcce3e)). Wise bans illustration from transfer setup, fee breakdown, review, and KYC entirely ([confirm and send](https://mobbin.com/screens/d8b0deb6-b783-42ec-a12f-d9327cf7a180)). The Gen-Z register is bought back precisely by proving it knows when to shut up.

**4. The one-breath reveal.** Resolve the quiz into one qualitative sentence, spend the flow's single big visual moment on it, then dole out details one screen at a time. Headspace names an identity ("For Calm Explorers like you") and justifies it by echoing the user's own quiz answers as factor chips ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/31b21791-dec6-448a-8253-648f5ebbba3e)). Duolingo (mechanics) leads with one verdict sentence, caps the verdict screen at exactly three stat chips, and gives every further disclosure its own single-CTA screen ([reveal](https://mobbin.com/screens/e40fbb2d-6f69-4bb6-b27f-8cc5968c95c0), [reveal variant](https://mobbin.com/screens/c00bbcbd-f4c9-4b5c-acf0-00dd9be39813)). Wise spends its only full-bleed colour inversion on this beat — forest-green screen, "ALL SENT", one quiet exit pill ([success](https://mobbin.com/screens/e3c6bc82-2aa7-4cea-a6d6-619da43bf7d6)). Cleo stages its spending reveal on a navy set-piece, preceded by paced brand-voice loading bubbles ([insights](https://mobbin.com/screens/e71711d9-1e4b-4fb1-ae45-8322c40cb34f), [loading](https://mobbin.com/screens/fcafc3eb-e76c-4dc1-ac9f-fdab69f6d390)). One emotional beat, fully committed, then back to paper.

**5. Dignified exits.** Every ask ships a no-shame decline; every step ships an escape; errors correct without blame. Airbnb keeps "Save & exit" on every wizard step and does validation as a grayed Next button rather than red scolding, with loading shown as an ellipsis inside the same button ([wizard flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/ac0a721e-274d-4b18-97eb-403b4c59b394), [amenities step](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd25fb02-cbdd-4db6-bf46-0a90943c4308)). Duolingo (mechanics) declines optional asks with "NOT NOW"/"LATER", answers a wrong answer with the correction and a low-blame "GOT IT", and defers the retry to a labelled "PREVIOUS MISTAKE" later in the session — never a forced redo ([lesson flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/706ba483-579e-4628-9903-ba0809870d18)). Cleo pairs its credit pitch with the decline "I don't like change" and offers "I need more info on security" as a first-class button in bank linking ([Unf*ck your credit flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/fd2ac5cb-3500-45ab-a9a6-27cb3970e71f), [bank linking flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/20e91726-9162-4513-8cad-bac3901046c8)). Wise's biometric consent carries an honest "No, check manually" opt-out ([verify flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/898f1907-18b8-4c5a-929a-e24227d5a193)); Notion keeps "Skip" available on every onboarding question ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/2ada33fc-fedc-4d26-a89a-96714e261e81)).

### Three tells of premium fintech-calm

**1. One loud pixel.** The entire saturation budget is spent on a single action per screen; everything else separates by hairline and tint. Linear budgets exactly one violet element — the CTA — with all other structure done in hairline borders and 2–3% tint shifts, and colour otherwise confined to 12px chips ([Plans](https://mobbin.com/screens/3b233553-84e9-4c59-adf7-e8ec5991b245), [issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4e0a8da-a387-49c7-8940-b64521bd3809)). Airbnb holds its pink-gradient CTA for money/identity commits only, running every workhorse control in black ([Request to book](https://mobbin.com/screens/e631293a-1f1d-47bf-a68d-72e63d9bb663), [wizard flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/ac0a721e-274d-4b18-97eb-403b4c59b394)). Wise role-locks neon green to one or two contained pills carrying dark text while ~95% of a transactional screen stays white ([fee flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/09656b3a-81bd-41ee-ae4c-22ef47c82716)); Notion's only saturated colour is the "you can act here" blue ([plan chooser](https://mobbin.com/screens/dd7ee17a-6f75-451e-9913-4dd61f03062f)).

**2. The number is the biggest thing on the page.** The user's data is the hero typography, and the chrome around it goes quiet — dominance of the figure is itself the trust claim. Cash App strips its keypad of all container chrome so a giant numeral is the only possible focal point ([contribute keypad](https://mobbin.com/screens/ea924c63-806f-41be-b479-4e7eb9d64ecd), [transfer flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/fede6d81-8d6f-4c65-aca3-9b524a9bf2ad)). Wise sets "You send exactly / Recipient gets" as ~40–48px forest-green numerals with fees, locked rate, and arrival time disclosed above the fold and before commitment ([fee flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/09656b3a-81bd-41ee-ae4c-22ef47c82716)). Airbnb renders the host's price as a 64–72px editable numeral beside a "you earn $97" receipt card ([wizard flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/ac0a721e-274d-4b18-97eb-403b4c59b394)); Phantom's home is a giant balance numeral on a 4-step dark ramp ([home](https://mobbin.com/screens/fb83b3b9-79fe-4fe6-821c-ff2ed3da9e42)).

**3. Ceremony is a full stop.** Success is one wholesale screen change to a single mark plus a plain sentence — never confetti layered onto existing UI — and machine strings borrow document seriousness. Phantom replaces the entire sheet with one centered check ("It's done!" / "Sent!") ([It's done!](https://mobbin.com/screens/70d96f2f-af1e-468b-9c85-2da5bc5c716a), [Sent!](https://mobbin.com/screens/61648374-83df-4f0e-8ef9-6b591c9d164a)). Cash App hard-cuts to a mostly-empty white screen with a check circle and a plain deposit sentence ([transfer flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/fede6d81-8d6f-4c65-aca3-9b524a9bf2ad)); Revolut is anti-celebratory even at account funding — a small blue check in a dark sheet ([funded account](https://mobbin.com/screens/d9964d71-7238-41de-a26d-f329199a8d36)). Wise commits fully but exactly once, and extends the same calm treatment to a cancellation ([ALL SENT](https://mobbin.com/screens/e3c6bc82-2aa7-4cea-a6d6-619da43bf7d6), [cancel confirm](https://mobbin.com/screens/fc1cca97-f528-4744-a376-1ca540798052)). The mono tell rides along: Linear's login code field ([login flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/2b9e0315-3654-482e-b877-f8da3736939f)) and Cash App's typewriter legal fine print ([disclosures](https://mobbin.com/screens/01ea9d8c-189a-4ce4-a952-c9c829b8cfde)) both use mono to signal "this is the machine speaking precisely".

### For MV-83 (palette candidates)

- **① Night indigo — supported but crowded.** Phantom proves a single desaturated periwinkle (~#AB9FF2, deliberately never neon) can carry an entire brand on cool dark neutrals as both mark and action colour ([splash](https://mobbin.com/screens/59958f74-06d2-4804-b81d-6d88c2cbf057), [onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/e5a64e8a-71a7-4962-98b0-30f3e4c05c01)), and Linear proves muted indigo-violet (~#5E6AD2) reads calm-premium in *light* mode when confined to one CTA per screen ([theme picker + Continue](https://mobbin.com/screens/efc1c7a1-c215-4de7-be32-4f68a1bb3e20)). Warning: this is the most-claimed hue space in the set — Linear owns muted indigo in productivity, Phantom owns lavender in crypto, Cleo's electric blurple and Revolut's cobalt sit adjacent. Ownability depends on a distinct saturation/temperature position, not the hue alone. Dark-mode derivation is the cheapest of the three (Phantom is dark-native proof the hue survives near-black); the light half must lean on Linear's evidence, including its disabled-state rule (desaturate further to pale lavender, [disabled Continue](https://mobbin.com/screens/fa5eecc4-532f-4cc2-a6e8-56814eefbf89)).
- **② Deep blue — the evidence warns hardest here.** Across the set, blue is the default *functional* colour, not a brand: Notion's ~#2383E2 strictly means "you can act here" ([plan chooser](https://mobbin.com/screens/dd7ee17a-6f75-451e-9913-4dd61f03062f)), Headspace's interactive primary is royal blue while orange carries the brand ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/31b21791-dec6-448a-8253-648f5ebbba3e)), and Airbnb reserves blue for legal links only ([login flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/c5d1a85f-504c-428e-8c3f-2750b2fa520d)). A blue brand primary camouflages against the universal link/button convention — the corporate-generic risk is confirmed, not just suspected. Revolut shows the only observed rescue: split the roles — deep blue→black gradient washes at boundary/hero moments only, flat bright blue demoted to links, primary CTA flipped to white/black pills ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/835f959d-7928-45a7-a4f7-fdec964e7270), [Metal flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/8e6a9960-5935-4eb7-8f36-cc037e8438da)). That discipline is expensive and still reads fintech-common.
- **③ Dusk plum — most ownable, claim holds.** No app in the ten occupies deep plum/berry; nearest neighbours are lighter and cooler (Phantom lavender) or electric (Cleo blurple, [setup flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/78850b24-4ac4-4c5a-ae91-89014abd6d4d)). Warm sand paper has precedent — Headspace's warm off-white (~#F5F1EC) pills/cards and Notion's greige sidebar both read calm, not cheap ([Today feed](https://mobbin.com/screens/efc82d96-660f-4e91-bff9-1f3d083c34eb), [onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/2ada33fc-fedc-4d26-a89a-96714e261e81)) — but note every fintech in the set runs cool neutrals (Wise, Revolut, Cash App, Phantom, Linear), so warm sand pulls toward wellness/editorial; test a plum-on-cooler-paper variant against the fintech-calm tells before locking. Dark mode: follow Wise's tinted-near-black precedent (green-tinted near-black, [dark Payments](https://mobbin.com/screens/65a4eba8-dd4e-4ed5-9e19-431b6c5af661)) — a plum-tinted near-black with a re-picked ramp.
- **Saturation strategy (all candidates):** desaturate the primary and role-lock any full-saturation use to contained fills carrying dark text. Phantom is deliberately never-neon; Linear dampens to periwinkle; Wise's neon green is fill-only — banned as text or background on transactional surfaces, always carrying dark ink ([fee flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/09656b3a-81bd-41ee-ae4c-22ef47c82716)).
- **Neutral ramp:** 4–6 distinguishable steps per mode is the universal count (Phantom 4–5 dark; Linear 4–5 light; Notion 5–6; Airbnb 5–6; Wise 4 light; Revolut 4–5 dark / 3–4 light). Recommend a 5-step light ramp plus an independently re-picked 4–5-step dark ramp — every dual-mode app in the set re-picks its dark palette rather than inverting (Linear [preferences](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4e47b31-9152-40c7-a400-c6725e8de647), Notion [dark preferences](https://mobbin.com/screens/d138214d-5351-4bd6-a2bb-c7b3648ea6a5), Wise, Revolut).
- **Accent-count discipline:** 2–3 functional accents beyond the primary, confined to micro scale. Wise runs effectively zero beyond its two greens; Cash App two (error red + a domain colour); Linear tolerates 5–6 hues only because none ever exceeds a 12px chip ([issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4e0a8da-a387-49c7-8940-b64521bd3809)).
- **Verdict trio coexistence:** the Strong/Possible/Reach trio *is* the accent budget, so the primary must stay off the green–amber–red axis (all three candidates comply). Keep verdict hues at word+band scale like Linear's status chips, never as large fills. Critical split: destructive/error red must stay visually separable from Reach red — Wise and Cash App spend red exclusively on genuine failure, which is what keeps it credible ([OTP error](https://mobbin.com/screens/2b9e5c52-3307-429d-90d0-35b70dcbf513), [failed payment](https://mobbin.com/screens/5e5322ef-e6d2-41eb-a850-c5ca521d9f9a)); if Reach borrows the exact error red, an ambitious option reads as a broken one.

### For MV-84 (design-system v2)

- **Radius scale:** the set converges on a two-register system — tight rects for inputs (~6–12px), medium cards (12–20px), sheets/panels 16–28px top corners, and full 999px pills for buttons/chips. Two apps deliberately split pill vs rect by ceremony: Linear uses pills only outside the app shell and ~6px rects inside it ([theme step](https://mobbin.com/screens/efc1c7a1-c215-4de7-be32-4f68a1bb3e20)); Airbnb uses pills for chrome/utility and 8–12px rects for form CTAs ([wizard flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/ac0a721e-274d-4b18-97eb-403b4c59b394)). MyVisa's frozen 8/12/16/999 scale conforms; the one evidenced addition is a 24–28px sheet-top tier (Wise, Revolut, Cleo, Headspace all sit there).
- **Type scale/weights:** a single grotesque with exactly two working weights carries hierarchy everywhere — Phantom regular+semibold, Cash App bold+regular, Headspace bold+regular, Airbnb bold + gray value ([home balance](https://mobbin.com/screens/fb83b3b9-79fe-4fe6-821c-ff2ed3da9e42), [onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/52c01e84-1cf8-4268-a89e-6ab01327d2c0)). Hierarchy = size jumps + gray value, not weight variety; Linear proves the compressed alternative (weight + gray inside 12–24px, [issue list](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4e0a8da-a387-49c7-8940-b64521bd3809)). Mono is reserved for machine strings only (Linear login code [login flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/2b9e0315-3654-482e-b877-f8da3736939f); Cash App legal fine print [disclosures](https://mobbin.com/screens/01ea9d8c-189a-4ce4-a952-c9c829b8cfde)) — matches the IBM Plex Mono role. The fintech "display numeral tier" (40–96px: Wise amounts, Cash App keypad, Airbnb price) maps for MyVisa to verdict-band typography, not numbers — we never show percentages.
- **Motion vocabulary observed → planned CSS vocabulary:** (a) content swap inside fixed chrome (Airbnb wizard [flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/ac0a721e-274d-4b18-97eb-403b4c59b394); Headspace domed sheet [flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/31b21791-dec6-448a-8253-648f5ebbba3e); Notion onboarding) = slide-fwd/slide-back; (b) small in-place state flips ("Copy"→"Copied" Phantom [copy flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/68603494-153b-4b5b-94ff-3b7b5d8fc4f7); gray→black CTA Cash App [amount states](https://mobbin.com/screens/fbabc1bb-a13a-4047-ad28-84b59428a9a7); disabled→active→ellipsis-loading Next, Airbnb [amenities](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd25fb02-cbdd-4db6-bf46-0a90943c4308)) = rise-sm scale changes; (c) bottom sheets over a dimmed scrim as the default modal grammar (Phantom, Revolut [onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/835f959d-7928-45a7-a4f7-fdec964e7270), Notion, Cleo); (d) exactly one wholesale full-screen change per flow, reserved for the terminal reveal/success = the single --ease-out-emphatic reveal per page.
- **"One reveal moment per page" is proven by:** Wise — its only full-bleed inversion in the entire app is the success screen ([ALL SENT](https://mobbin.com/screens/e3c6bc82-2aa7-4cea-a6d6-619da43bf7d6)); Phantom — whole-sheet reset to a centered check ([It's done!](https://mobbin.com/screens/70d96f2f-af1e-468b-9c85-2da5bc5c716a)); Cash App — hard cut to check + sentence ([transfer flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/fede6d81-8d6f-4c65-aca3-9b524a9bf2ad)); Revolut goes even quieter (a sheet, [funded account](https://mobbin.com/screens/d9964d71-7238-41de-a26d-f329199a8d36)). Duolingo's post-lesson chain is the sequenced form: one disclosure per screen, one CTA each ([reveal](https://mobbin.com/screens/e40fbb2d-6f69-4bb6-b27f-8cc5968c95c0)).
- **Staggers:** sequential disclosure is the observed idiom — Cleo's paced brand-voice loading bubbles ([loading](https://mobbin.com/screens/fcafc3eb-e76c-4dc1-ac9f-fdab69f6d390)) and Duolingo's one-stat-per-screen chain. No app showed simultaneous multi-element choreography on data surfaces, so ≤5 siblings at 40–60ms is consistent with the evidence but not directly measurable — all ten sections are stills; durations/easing were unobservable everywhere. Treat the planned timings as our own spec, validated only in kind.
- **Loading:** the calmest observed loading state lives *inside* the button (Airbnb's three-dot ellipsis in the black Next, [amenities step](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd25fb02-cbdd-4db6-bf46-0a90943c4308)) — adopt for wizard/plan actions. No skeleton screens were captured anywhere in the set; no evidence either way.

### For MV-85 (mascot brief)

- **Where the character belongs — shell and boundary surfaces only**, which matches the founder-locked list exactly: splash/welcome and identity moments (Phantom ghost — [splash](https://mobbin.com/screens/59958f74-06d2-4804-b81d-6d88c2cbf057), [welcome](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd29d474-40fa-4de2-9473-f74c3abb5b7e); Notion's only ink drawing is auth + role picker — [role picker](https://mobbin.com/screens/211e8a30-c2c6-44ac-a552-cddda1c54916)); empty states (Headspace's pink creature — [offline downloads](https://mobbin.com/screens/c344dbac-03c6-47c5-baad-42e0503cba0d); Wise's display-face empties — [empty recipients](https://mobbin.com/screens/edec2f84-aa94-4c01-9bd5-cdd275d833aa)); milestone/reveal beats (Headspace recommendation card — [onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/31b21791-dec6-448a-8253-648f5ebbba3e); Wise success art — [ALL SENT](https://mobbin.com/screens/e3c6bc82-2aa7-4cea-a6d6-619da43bf7d6)).
- **Where it must pointedly not appear — the strongest finding in the study:** all eight styled apps remove character/illustration from money, security, compliance, and assessment surfaces without exception. Phantom: send/PIN/recovery/settings ([send keypad](https://mobbin.com/screens/47237839-b82f-430d-add2-a42c46c80b3f)); Headspace: sign-up form, the whole 10-question assessment, and — sharpest precedent for verdicts — the results score, which is a plain numeric disc on a Low/Moderate/High band with zero character ([check-in flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/ce032b9a-cb9f-42f9-b407-19a2796c6961)); Cleo: Plaid/KYC/payment ([Plaid credentials](https://mobbin.com/screens/ff64faaa-447a-4279-8d63-049b01c20a19)); Wise: transfer/fees/confirm/KYC ([confirm and send](https://mobbin.com/screens/d8b0deb6-b783-42ec-a12f-d9327cf7a180)); Revolut and Cash App keep money surfaces type-only ([Manage plan](https://mobbin.com/screens/c9545ed1-3955-43cc-9fca-0796b6796b0e), [contribute keypad](https://mobbin.com/screens/ea924c63-806f-41be-b479-4e7eb9d64ecd)). "Never on data surfaces, never beside a verdict" is not house taste; it is the industry-wide trust mechanic.
- **Error zoning is split, and the split matters:** micro glyphs are acceptable on full-page dead-ends (Notion's drawn-eyes on [content does not exist](https://mobbin.com/screens/21fade9f-d41a-4ee5-8052-4d2803765a21)), but inline/in-flow errors are text-only and joke-free everywhere (Notion's plain [inline error](https://mobbin.com/screens/e00ca642-dcfd-4797-aec7-9ae0d780ec3d); Cleo's straight, humour-free payment error banner [plan sheet](https://mobbin.com/screens/2b6390fa-0766-4449-8065-f8e8ed951c8a); Wise's small red inline [OTP error](https://mobbin.com/screens/2b9e5c52-3307-429d-90d0-35b70dcbf513)). The bird may hold a 404/dead-end page; it must never appear beside a validation or failure message inside a flow.
- **Cleo's personality-density lesson:** maximum personality (swearing, memes, second-person voice) coexists with financial trust only because it fully stands down at credential moments and pre-answers trust objections in plain terms ("Cleo can't move your money…", plus an "I need more info on security" button — [bank linking flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/20e91726-9162-4513-8cad-bac3901046c8)). Its one credibility risk is roast mode mocking the user's own behaviour ([roast chat](https://mobbin.com/screens/f3ed9a16-b926-45d9-b6fc-eecda7f863b5)) — direct grounding for the no-shame rule: the bird never comments on, reacts to, or jokes about the student's data or verdict.
- **Posture/abstraction:** Phantom's ghost is a flat abstract silhouette by default and gains a face + speech bubble only at the identity moment ([Public Addresses](https://mobbin.com/screens/f8cd29fb-f8fc-45e2-af01-42ecd83189d9)) — expressiveness should scale with the emotional license of the surface: fully geometric at rest (landing, empty states), most expressive only at identity/milestone beats. Airbnb, Revolut, and Wise prove object-grade illustration delivers empty-state warmth with no actor at all ([tilted 3D cards](https://mobbin.com/screens/e1d8aa60-cf3f-481a-922b-24afaed2cac9), [puzzle empty](https://mobbin.com/screens/f7d90e37-7632-411e-bfb9-8e555a852962)) — so the bird should behave as a composed object in the scene, not a performer: no eyes tracking numbers, no pointing at CTAs or verdict bands.
- **Duolingo's mechanics-vs-style separation:** every transferable completion mechanic — progress filling through signup, verdict-first reveal, labelled deferred retries — works with zero character celebration ([onboarding flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/b0b4f93f-5637-46ec-9d77-49ecda6b991d), [lesson flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/706ba483-579e-4628-9903-ba0809870d18)). The bird is not needed to drive completion; never deploy it as a reward dispenser.
- **The never-do list, grounded:** never on wizard question screens or forms (Airbnb uses thin line icons only; Headspace and Cleo go plain); never beside a verdict (Headspace score-disc precedent); never in inline errors (universal); never in confetti-mascot success combos — the premium apps succeed with a markless check or object art, not a cheering character (Phantom, Cash App, Revolut, Wise above); never delivering security warnings — Phantom uses a padlock object and plain icons for those, not the ghost ([recovery phrase flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/f9206cbe-976b-48b9-9112-6ed7fcc0dd03)).
