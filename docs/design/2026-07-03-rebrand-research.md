## Method

Live Mobbin MCP sweep (fresh session, tools verified 2026-07-03). One research agent per app examined actual Mobbin screenshots — every observation cites the screen it came from (`mobbin.com/screens/...`). Ten apps: Phantom (the pole), Linear, Headspace, Airbnb, Notion, Wise, Revolut, Cleo, Cash App, and Duolingo (**flow mechanics only** — its visual style is explicitly out of scope per the card). Surfaces swept per app: onboarding quiz/wizard, sign-up/auth, results/score reveal, conversion prompts, empty states, error states, dark-mode handling.

Honesty rules applied throughout: durations are only reported where evident (Mobbin returns stills — most timing notes are choreography inferences, marked as such); coverage gaps are declared per app rather than guessed around.
